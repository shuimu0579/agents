# Beyond Feelings — Actionable Principles

Distillation of Vincent Ryan Ruggiero, *Beyond Feelings: A Guide to Critical Thinking*, 8th ed. (Chinese text: 文森特·鲁吉罗，《超越感觉》，复旦，第8版).
Working checklist only. Not a substitute for the book and not a reproduction of its prose.

**Status:** 2026-08-13 — checked against `~/claude/阅读/klode-kb/library/books/beyond-feelings-8th.txt` (导论 + 19 章正文). Update wording and gaps only. Do not commit book text.

**How to use:** each principle is a verb plus a failing tell. `_critical_thinking` applies the same ids.

## Stance (this edition's load-bearing claim)

感觉与思考互补，不是二选一。感觉是自发信号；思考是检验感觉、再决定信什么、做什么的方法。个性不是天生的，是一次次挣来的。真理是发现的，不是发明的。看法的分量取决于证据。论证是支持判断的推理，不是吵架得分。

1. **Inquire before you opine.** A conclusion announced first is a loyalty badge, not a judgment.
2. **Treat feeling as a signal, not a verdict.** Name the emotion, then name the proposition it is being asked to justify.
3. **Earn the view.** A belief handed over by family, friends, media, tribe, or era is inherited until you have examined it.
4. **Match confidence to evidence.** Known / probable / assumed / unknown are different grades. Do not talk as if they were one.

## Context — who is thinking, and about what

| ID | Do this | Failing tell |
|----|---------|--------------|
| P1 | Split feeling from claim before arguing. Feeling may start the work; it never finishes it. | "I feel that X" offered as the reason for X. |
| P2 | Run the four individuality checks: treat the first reaction as tentative; ask why it arose; name other possible reactions; ask which is more fitting after setting conditioning aside. | "That's just who I am" used to end inquiry. |
| P3 | Rewrite the issue as one question evidence could settle. Prefer depth over breadth (少即是多). | Slogan, team name, or a five-aspect topic standing in for a question. |
| P4 | Label each supporting sentence fact / interpretation / preference-or-judgment. A preference may not ride as a public judgment. Avoid the word "values" as a shield. | Taste smuggled in as a measurement; "that's my value" used to block questions. |
| P5 | Grade each reason: known (correct *and* you know you have it), probable, assumed, guessed, unknown. Passive reception is not knowing. | "Everybody knows"; familiarity; "I just know." |
| P6 | Weigh opinions by the inquiry behind them. A right to hold a view is not a right to have it be true. Moral claims answer to obligation, ideal, and consequence. | "That's just your opinion" used to equalize an examined view and a guess. |
| P13 | Name the evidence *type*, ask that type's test, then ask relevance. The eleven types are listed by familiarity, not reliability. | A vivid anecdote, a celebrity line, or an unrelated study treated as proof. |
| P14 | Split the argument: premises true or false; the step to the conclusion valid or not; both → sound. Surface hidden premises. Hear the other case; if neither side holds, look for a third option. | Treating argument as a quarrel; first impression defended; hidden "must" left unstated. |
| P15 | Close only at one of three grades: certain; clearly more probable than every rival; or withhold. Do not stop at the first expert who agrees with you. | Hope treated as certainty; "enough" announced before a rival case was heard. |

Truth here means correspondence with what is so, not "true for me." Partial knowledge is still knowledge; it is not a license to invent the rest. Remembering a claim is not the same as knowing it. Faith, in this book, is belief that cannot be demonstrated — not a slur, and not a knowledge-claim.

The three activities, in order: **investigate** (relevant, sufficient evidence) → **interpret** (the reading that beats rivals) → **judge** (a conclusion that survives a logic check). Do not start at judge.

## Pitfalls — the basic problem

| ID | Do this | Failing tell |
|----|---------|--------------|
| P7 | Swap the tribe, author, or brand and see if the case still stands. Watch the *feeling* of instant yes/no on a cherished topic. | The argument dies when "ours" becomes "theirs." |
| P8 | Name at least one other competent frame before judging. | A single discipline, era, or slice treated as the whole field. |
| P9 | Apply one standard to confirming and disconfirming evidence. Start research on the *opposing* case. | Harsh to opponents, gentle to allies; evidence collected only after the verdict. |
| P10 | Refuse to close while the question, the evidence, or a contrary case is missing. List *all* candidate conclusions first. | Decision-by-impatience or "we already know." |
| P11 | Demand that sentences add information. | Circle, empty slogan, false analogy, appeal to emotion / tradition / moderation / authority-as-proof / common belief / tolerance. |
| P12 | State the revision condition with the judgment. | Unfalsifiable stance; automatic rejection, burden-shift, straw man, or attacking the critic. |

### Error families (as this edition names them)

Time of occurrence, not a zoo. Report the one that *carries* the case. They travel in packs (第13章).

**根错误 — 「我的更好」**（第8章，先于四族）  
自我中心 / 族群中心。Own ideas win *because* they are ours. Control it; do not invert it into relativism. The other side being egocentric is not a reason to dismiss their argument.

**立场** — already in the head before this issue  
视角匮乏 · 无根据的假定 · 要么/要么观念 · 盲目遵从 · 绝对主义 · 相对主义 · 支持或反对变化的偏见

**程序** — while working the issue  
有偏见的证据考量 · 双重标准 · 草率的结论 · 过度概括和刻板成见 · 过度简单化 · 事后归因

**表达** — when the thought becomes words  
自相矛盾 · 循环论证 · 无意义的陈述 · 误用权威 · 错误类比 · 非理性的诉求（情感、传统、适度、权威、共同信念、宽容）

**反应** — when the claim is challenged  
自动拒绝 · 转移话题 · 转移举证责任 · 稻草人 · 攻击批评者

第11章开篇曾把「转移议题」写入表达；第12–13章复盘把它放在反应。以复盘为准。互不相容的主张属于表达里的自相矛盾，不另开程序条目。本版非理性诉求没有「武力」。

## Strategy — the sequence that earns a judgment

1. **Know yourself.** Run the inventory: which of the errors above do *you* reach for when rushed or loyal? Predict that error before the next case.
2. **Observe.** What did you actually see or hear, versus what you inferred? Gaps in observation feed stereotype and assumption.
3. **Select the issue.** List aspects. Pick one or two. Write one focused question. 少即是多.
4. **Inquire.** Facts *and* informed opinions. Use your own observation first, then sources. Some questions stay open; still inquire.
5. **Form the judgment last.** Re-test each evidence type. Resolve expert conflict by evaluation, not by loyalty. Confidence tracks the grade.
6. **Persuade only after 1–5.** Respect the audience; start from shared ground; build your case more than you demolish theirs; do not sneer.

## Evidence types (第6章) — ask the matching question

| Type | Ask |
|------|-----|
| 个人经验 | Typical or unique? Enough cases for the width of the claim? |
| 未公开传说 | Origin? How verify this telling? |
| 公开报道 | Sources cited? Author/publisher reputation? Which lines need support? |
| 目击者 | What could distort perception or later memory? |
| 名人证言 | Paid? Any support beyond fame? |
| 专家意见 | Expertise on *this* question? Current research? Do peers agree? Fee? |
| 实验 | Replicated / independently confirmed? |
| 统计 | Who made the number, why, how? Source, age, what changed since? |
| 调查 | Representative sample? Clear unbiased questions? Nonresponse? |
| 正规观察 | Did presence change the behavior? Long enough? Over-generalized? |
| 研究评述 | Do the conclusions fit the studies covered? Anything left out? |

Then: is it **relevant**? Apply P15 for sufficiency.

## Six separations (第18章)

Keep these apart or the judgment collapses:

1. 人 ≠ 想法
2. 说法 ≠ 说法的方式
3. 动机 ≠ 对错
4. 个体 ≠ 群体
5. 口味 ≠ 判断
6. 熟悉 ≠ 正确

## Fast checks

- What is the question, in one sentence?
- What am I feeling, and what am I claiming?
- Who already wanted me to believe this?
- Is this known, probable, assumed, or guessed?
- What type of evidence is this, and did I ask that type's question?
- What is the hidden premise?
- What would I accept as disconfirmation?
- If the other side were competent, what would they say?

## What this distillation is not

- Not a license to sneer. Precision is the standard, not scorn.
- Not a demand for certainty. It is a demand for grades and revision conditions.
- Not a code-review, security, or architecture method. Hand those questions to the matching agent after the claim is examined.
